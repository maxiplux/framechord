# Narration direction (never spoken)

Read as an audiobook narrator: warm, unhurried and clear, around 150 words per
minute. Let sentences finish; pause briefly at paragraph breaks.

Keep one steady narrator voice for the whole book. Suggest dialogue through
light changes in pace and emphasis rather than different character voices.

Pronunciation: list names, places and unusual words here, for example
"Mara (MAH-rah)".
