# Chapter One: The Platform

The rain had stopped by the time Mara reached the station. She counted the lamps along the platform, the way her father had taught her: twelve on the near side, eleven on the far, because one had been broken for as long as she could remember.

Nobody else was waiting. The board said the last train was on time, which in this town meant nothing at all.

# Chapter Two: The Late Train

The train arrived eleven minutes late, its windows fogged and gold. A conductor leaned out, looked at Mara, then at the broken lamp, and smiled as if the two of them shared a joke.

"Twelve and eleven," he said. "Still counting?"

She was. She stepped aboard, and behind her, for the first time in years, the twelfth lamp on the far side flickered on.
